## About me

I am a first-semester Computer Science student at **Universidade Cidade de Sao Paulo**. I was born in Colombia and now live in Brazil, and I am still at the very beginning of my programming journey.

Before starting university I worked with commercial and business data, and that is what sparked my interest in data: turning plain numbers into something people can actually use to make decisions. I do not know much yet, and I am happy to say so. This profile is where I document what I learn along the way.

<br>

## What I am exploring

- **Data** is the area that interests me most, even though I am only starting to learn the basics.
- **Programming fundamentals** through C, which is helping me understand how computers work underneath.
- **Databases and SQL**, because good data starts with good structure.
- **Front-end basics** with HTML and CSS, to understand how the things I use every day are built.

## Featured project

### Gym ERP System

*Academic team project, in development*

A management system for a gym, built around a relational database. It is the most complete repository I have so far, and it taught me more than any single lecture:

- How to model real-world information into tables and relationships.
- How to turn business rules (memberships, payments, schedules) into logic.
- How to work with a team using Git and GitHub.

[View the repository](https://github.com/jdcasanov7/Holdfit_ERP)
